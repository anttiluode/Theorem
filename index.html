Why this matters

This pattern returns to the same value after the long shift. Two different operators therefore give identical outputs on it.

Mathematically the difference is 0.1 × (shift(+17,−9) − identity). Its gain for a Fourier mode is 0.2 × |sin(π(17ky − 9kx)/64)|. The mode indices form a lattice of 64 exactly dark and 64 maximally bright frequencies out of 4096.
